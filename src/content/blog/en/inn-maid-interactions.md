Added two features related to the inn maid.

## Food Orders

You can order food from the maid at the inn. The maid takes the order and responds.

![Ordering food from the maid on the first floor of the inn](../../../assets/blog/inn-food-order.png)

## Second-Floor Greeting

When a character wakes up on the second floor of the inn after dying, the maid moves upstairs. She greets the character after they wake up.

![The maid greeting a character who woke up on the second floor of the inn](../../../assets/blog/inn-second-floor-wakeup.png)
